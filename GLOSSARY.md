# Glossary

The shared language for this project. Domain terms only — no implementation
details, no specs.

## Client

The business owner who commissions and pays for the website. The sole
decision-maker for design approval and for the accuracy of project claims.

## Project

A real development the Client is selling: land, approvals, units, a handover
date. A Project exists independently of the website. Only real Projects may
appear on the site — a marketing card is not a Project.

## Flagship project

The single Project the Client is currently selling. It is the centrepiece of
the site at launch.

## Promoters' track record

Verifiable prior developments delivered by the Client or the promoters under
another entity. May be shown with clear attribution. Unverifiable work is not
track record.

## Mockup

A design-approval artefact: the real page structure and layout filled with
placeholder text, images and a placeholder logo. Not a launch-ready site, and
not indexed by search engines.

## Design approval

The Client's written acceptance of the mockup's look and structure. It closes
the mockup phase and starts the final build. One consolidated round of design
changes is included before approval.

## Launch (go-live)

The production site published on the Client's own domain with real content,
working enquiry channels, SSL and legal pages.

## Enquiry

A sales lead arriving through one of three channels: website form, WhatsApp
click-to-chat, or phone-call click.

## Project page

A page dedicated to one Project, carrying its facts, gallery, location and
enquiry call-to-action.

## Care plan

The monthly service that keeps the live site current: content changes, backups,
SSL, uptime checks and a monthly report.

## Pragna standard

The reference bar set by pragnagroup.co.in: the same page structure and a
premium, photography-led feel. It does not include Webflow animations, custom
illustration, or per-project microsites.
